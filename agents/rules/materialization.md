# Materialization — preserve valuable talk as notes

_Generated by LLM(Claude Opus 4.8): 2026-06-20 (v0.0.10-min); Approved by human?: NOT YET;_

> Always-on capture: **valuable chat content must be persisted to disk as a note** — chat is transport,
> not storage. Sibling of the track ([`../sessions.md`](../sessions.md) §3). Load on any session — not
> opt-in. Rationale (not loaded): [`../rationale/rules/materialization.md`](../rationale/rules/materialization.md).

## Summary

- **What.** The **valuable, complete** content — a substantive answer/insight from the assistant, or an
  ad-hoc attestation/reasoning from the human. **Not** intermittent chatter or in-flight back-and-forth.
- **Where.** A note under the focused topic's **`notes/talk/`** (`topics/<topic>/notes/talk/`).
- **Why.** Chat is ephemeral; anything worth keeping is on disk or it does not exist.

## What counts as "valuable"

| Materialize | Skip |
|---|---|
| A complete, substantive answer/insight from the assistant | Intermittent messages, acknowledgements, status pings |
| An ad-hoc attestation, decision, or reasoning from the human | In-flight back-and-forth still converging |
| Something a future session needs and couldn't reconstruct from artifacts | Anything already in an artifact (track, report, the chain) |

## How

- **Default home:** `topics/<topic>/notes/talk/` — one note per coherent piece, named for its subject;
  follow [`doc-format.md`](doc-format.md).
- **Promptly.** Materialize as the content is produced, not only at wrap-up — so nothing is lost to an
  interrupted session.
- **Relation to the method.** These notes land in `notes/`, so they are ordinary inputs note-briefing
  ([`../methods/brainstorm.md`](../methods/brainstorm.md)) surfaces and SDLC later processes.
