# Revalidation rules

_Generated by LLM(Claude Opus 4.8): 2026-06-20 (v0.0.10-min); Approved by human?: YES;_

> The revalidation handover: a session that produced cognitive-heavy output is followed by one whose job
> is to **check it**. Load when writing a revalidation successor prompt or acting as the revalidating
> agent. 

## When to apply

Structure the successor as a revalidation task when the completed task:

- applied a multi-step transformation to existing material, **or**
- produced substantive new content through judgment, **or**
- generated artifacts downstream tasks will trust and build on without re-reading the source.

**Not** for purely structural/mechanical tasks (renames, reorg, rule/prompt authoring) — those get a
wait-for-human placeholder ([`../sessions.md`](../sessions.md) §5).

## Checklist

Work through all; frame findings as a structured report (confirm clean, call out corrections with
file/section refs). Do **not** proceed to the next substantive task without human approval.

1. **Intent reconstruction.** Read the commissioning prompt; state in one sentence what the task was to
   achieve.
2. **Intent-vs-outcome coherence** (highest-order; run before mechanical checks):
   - **a) Internal coherence** — output hangs together with itself.
   - **b) Coherence toward intent** — serves the goal from check 1, not just the stated steps.
   - **c) Coherence toward surrounding context** — fits adjacent unchanged artifacts (no contradiction;
     consistent vocabulary/abstraction).
   - **d) Updated-surroundings self-coherence** — if it edited surrounding docs, those are still
     internally consistent with accurate cross-refs.
3. **Consistency check.** Transformation applied uniformly? No residual pre-transform artifacts;
   spot-check multiple files; a rule applies everywhere it should.
4. **Integrity check.** What should be preserved was? No content silently dropped; cross-links intact;
   substance not distorted (abstraction fine, distortion not); markers coherent.
5. **Gap check.** Calibrated? **Under-done** (should have transformed but didn't) vs **over-done**
   (abstracted past usefulness, scope exceeded).
6. **Artifact check.** All expected outputs present and complete? (no missing/empty/truncated; no
   leftover placeholders.)

## After the checks

1. **Report findings clearly** — confirm clean; call out corrections with specific refs.
2. **Make or explicitly propose corrections** — don't leave the state ambiguous.
3. **Ask the human before proceeding** — errors here propagate downstream.
