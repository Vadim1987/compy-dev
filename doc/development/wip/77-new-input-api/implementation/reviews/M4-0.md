The slice does what M4-0 must do: it lands a green characterization net at keypress granularity over
the **production** `love.handlers.*` slots, carries the one forward `isrepeat` assertion as a `pending`
(not a red), respects the scope fence (tests-only, `src/` untouched, `editor_session.lua` unchanged),
and the teeth are real — I independently reproduced a perturb→red→restore, and the suite counts match
the ledger. It is a sound guardrail for the **controller-level** rework M4–M7 perform.

**Approval-scope note.** What the evidence establishes: the net has genuine teeth against the two
production behaviours M4–M7 will actually touch — (1) handler→`love.*` routing, and (2) the
`UserInputController` + `InputEvalText` overlay submit/cancel/`is_empty` path. What it does **not**
establish, and remains an open gate: per-example behaviour (notably the `keyboard` debounce, which is
**reimplemented in-test**, not characterized from the example), and the maze Lua-command path
(uncharacterizable black-box). Plus one structural wart: the new `input_session.lua` driver is **dead
code** — the net inlines a verbatim copy instead of consuming it. None of these block M4; all are
logged in `technical_debt.md` for M4/M8 pickup.
