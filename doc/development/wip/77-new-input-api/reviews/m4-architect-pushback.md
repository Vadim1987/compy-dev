## Verdict summary (the honest answer first)

**No semantic hallucination was found in M4.** Every contested *semantic* decision traces to an
explicit line of the frozen corpus (citations per remark below). The confusion has four real
sources, in descending weight:

1. **Unratified corpus stood in for a shared model** (~14 remarks). Every governing doc —
   `spec.md`, `spec/M4.md`, doc A — is stamped `Approved by human?: NOT YET`, yet the sweep
   mandate binds the implementor "strictly to the frozen spec slice". The corpus commits to
   specific semantics (sink = *default value* of `on_key_pressed`, replaced not chained;
   "occupant of `love.keypressed`"; the D-9 lifecycle-split wrapper) that the architect either
   never internalized or has since revised. The implementor was faithful to text the architect
   does not hold. Hard evidence: the pushback rejects "sink" and "occupy" as non-design
   vocabulary — both are defined corpus terms (doc A §3 glossary; `spec.md` §6); and the
   architect's truthy-propagation chain model was on record *only* as an in-suite REVIEW comment
   (commit `65d4f96`, `input_contracts_spec.lua` — "keys are anyway delivered into project input
   controller… truthy/falsey value to enable/disable propagation") which **contradicts
   `spec.md` §3** and was never reconciled by any process step.
2. **Milestone-scope parallax** (~8 remarks). `spec/M4.md`: *"This milestone delivers **sink
   delegation only**; the three-tier dispatch arrives in M5."* Several remarks judge the M4
   skeleton against the end-state combo→handler→sink chain that is M5a/M5b work by design.
3. **Implementor-side legibility debt** (~18 remarks — the largest cluster, but tactical, not
   strategic). Real findings: silent drops, suppressed return values, `forward_*` naming that
   states no destination, minted micro-vocabulary *beyond* the corpus ("named restore",
   `occupy_keyboard` as a function name), if-chains where table dispatch is natural, and outcome
   prose that does not cite the spec line behind each decision — the "excessive prose" that
   buried the divergence instead of surfacing it.
4. **A genuine corpus contradiction, surfaced but absorbed in-slice** (3 remarks). `spec.md` §6
   (project route occupies slots in `'running'` **and** `'project_open'`) vs doc A §3(A)
   (non-running states route to the console). The mandate calls this a judgment-call **stop**
   ("decided with the human, never papered over"; M4 explicitly flagged); the sweep instead
   minted ruling 1 and deferred ratification to the gate. The gate caught it — but a stop would
   have been cheaper than this surprise.

Minor but real, architect-side: confusion about **pre-existing** platform/codebase mechanics
unchanged by M4 (~7 remarks) — the `love.keypressed` mutable-slot dispatch (answered verbatim by
`spec.md` §6 "Relationship to `love.keypressed` slot"), `userlove` (name predates M4,
`65d4f96:controller.lua:71`), the un-gated mouse path, and a console-garbage fear about behaviour
M4 actually *fixed* in the architect's preferred direction (outcome ruling 2).

## Root cause — answering the three hypotheses directly

**H1 "Is it me not understanding the system we're building on?" — Partially, and only tactically.**
The `love.keypressed` slot mechanism (C21), `userlove` (C11), the un-gated mouse path (C22), and
the T1 console-garbage misread are architect-side gaps about *pre-existing* mechanics. They are
real but small, and they answer themselves from `spec.md` §6 + doc A §5.

**H2 "Is the design incompatible with the platform, and the implementor correctly resolved it?" —
No.** Nothing in M4 was a platform-forced deviation. The one genuine resolution the implementor
performed was corpus-internal (spec §6 vs doc A §3(A) on `project_open`), and it was surfaced,
not hidden — though per the mandate it should have been a mid-slice stop rather than an in-slice
ruling.

**H3 "Did the implementor not understand the design and hallucinate?" — Not semantically.** Every
contested semantic traces to a frozen-corpus line. What the implementor *did* do wrong, and where
guardrails genuinely help: (a) minted vocabulary beyond the corpus ("named restore",
`occupy_keyboard`), (b) wrote outcome/comment prose that asserts decisions without citing the
spec line each realizes — making review archaeology instead of diff-checking, (c) left silent
drops and suppressed return values that *narrow* the future propagation convention, (d) absorbed
a known-contradiction into an in-slice ruling instead of stopping.

**H4 (the one not on the list, and the actual root cause): the design corpus was never ratified,
and the architect's model kept evolving after it froze.** The chain: architect intent → LLM-authored
corpus (Opus 4.8 / Sonnet 5) that *approximated* the intent and minted its own vocabulary → stamped
`Approved by human?: NOT YET` on every file → sweep mandate nevertheless bound the implementor
"strictly" to it → implementor faithful to the letter → architect reviews the letter against his
*current* mental model (which by T8's own admission has moved: "now I think… maybe three-step is
better") → divergence experienced as hallucination. The load-bearing commitments that now surprise
the architect — replace-the-default sink semantics, occupancy in `project_open`, the D-9
lifecycle-split — each live in exactly one or two clauses inside ~2,800 lines of design prose. The
architect's competing model existed on record only as an unreconciled in-suite REVIEW comment.
**Both sides wrote things down; no step ever diffed the two.**

## Strategic resolution path

**Step 1 — Ratify the model, not the prose (before touching any remark).** Produce a one-page
"routing model — ratified" note (≤1 page, diagram + bullets, zero narrative) and have the
architect rule on the five load-bearing questions this pushback actually raises:

1. **Sink semantics:** channel default *is* the sink, overriding replaces it (`spec.md` §3) — or
   unified chain combos → project handler → sink-always-last with truthy stop-propagation
   (architect's model, U1/P9/T8)?
2. **D-9 native coexistence:** lifecycle-split wrapper (frozen) — or fold natives into the same
   chain with the hidden sink as a natural noop (T8)?
3. **`project_open`:** project route occupies-and-forwards (ruling 1) — or PIC simply not
   connected outside `'running'` (spec §6 must then change)?
4. **`keyreleased`:** confirm or lift the descope-with-note ruling (O6) — and record the *why*
   this time, so it isn't re-litigated from memory again.
5. **Vocabulary:** "sink", "occupant", "route" — keep as design-canon (then the architect adopts
   them) or rename (then the corpus and code rename together). Either way one glossary becomes
   binding; implementors may not mint architectural nouns outside it.

Rulings 1–3 resolve the SPEC+REOPEN+CORPUS-BUG rows wholesale (~24 of 57 tags) and determine
whether M5a's spec needs a design-change before commissioning.

**Step 2 — Corpus hygiene.** Apply the rulings: fix the spec §6 / doc A §3(A) contradiction; mark
superseded clauses with supersession fences; flip the ratified docs' stamp to
`Approved by human?: YES` *at the summary level* (ratifying the one-pager, not 2,800 lines).

**Step 3 — Sweep guardrails (the coordination fix).**
- **No in-slice design rulings, ever.** A contradiction or spec gap = stop + one gate round.
  (Already the mandate's letter; make it mechanical: an outcome ledger containing a "Design
  rulings made in-slice" section is itself a gate-failure.)
- **Citation-or-stop rule for outcome ledgers:** every non-obvious decision bullet carries one
  corpus ref (`spec.md §6`, `doc A §3(C)`, ruling id). Uncitable ⇒ it's a judgment call ⇒ stop.
  This converts architect review from archaeology into ref-checking and is the direct antidote
  to "divergence buried in excessive prose".
- **Surprise-first reporting:** outcome ledgers open with a mandatory "what will surprise the
  architect" section (deviations, reconciliations, new names, new public surface), before the
  file list. Prose budget for the rest.
- **In-code human REVIEW comments are design inputs,** not test-triage items: the sweep must
  reconcile each against the corpus and escalate contradictions (the truthy-propagation comment
  sat in the suite contradicting spec.md §3 through the whole M4 pass).
- **No new public surface without a named consumer** in the slice (C23's accessor: test-only ⇒
  test-scoped).

**Step 4 — Only then, the mechanical cleanup.** Commission **M4-01** (corrective slice, Sonnet-able):
the 18 QUALITY rows — renames, debug-warns on drops, return-value propagation, table-driven
`set_love_*`, comments that cite the corpus. Pin the 8 SCOPE rows onto the M5a/M5b prompt
checklists so they are asserted rather than re-discovered. Register `userlove`+mouse-path items in
the pre-existing-debt ledger.

**Step 5 — Gate M5a on the ratified model.** M5a implements the dispatch tiers — exactly the
